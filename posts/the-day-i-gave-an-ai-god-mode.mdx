The rule I set for myself was simple: no undo. Whatever it did, I would live
with until evening.

## What I expected

Something dramatic. A wiped directory, a config file mangled beyond repair, a
story worth telling at dinner.

## What actually happened

It renamed forty photographs correctly and then asked me, in a comment in a
file I had not opened, whether I wanted the originals kept.

That question bothered me more than any amount of damage would have.
